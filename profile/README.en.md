# FreeLog

FreeLog is a **digital resource distribution and hosting platform built on a theme-and-plugin (micro-frontend) architecture**. We believe digital content distribution should be open, composable, and automatable, so we have open-sourced the platform core and its supporting tools.

## What the Platform Does

FreeLog serves two types of users: **content creators** and **developers**.

**For content creators (no coding required):**
- Upload digital content such as novels, comics, images, audio, and video, and set your own pricing and authorization policies
- Choose ready-made themes and plugins to present your content, and build your own digital resource site without front-end development
- Use the platform for resource distribution, promotion, and hosting

**For developers:**
- **Themes**: complete front-end projects that determine a site's appearance and layout, such as novel, podcast, and comic themes
- **Plugins**: small components invoked by themes, such as music players and readers, mounted into pages through runtime APIs

Content creators provide content, developers provide themes and plugins, and the two are combined in the FreeLog runtime environment to form a complete digital resource site.

## Publish with CLI and Let Agents Do the Work

FreeLog provides the official CLI tool `@freelog-cli/cli`, covering the entire workflow from resource creation to going live:
[CLI documentation](https://widget-docs.freelog.cn)

The command-line nature of the CLI makes it naturally suited for invocation by scripts or AI agents. You can integrate FreeLog CLI into your agent workflows, letting agents automatically handle builds, version management, and publishing, reducing manual effort.
